Atlas of Colorado Project

[2/27/25]
This map is intended for tourists visiting Colorado to ski! The map shows all of the ski resorts in Colorado: whether they are on the IKON pass, the Epic pass, or an independent mountain. The map also shows nearby regional airports that someone may want to fly into instead of flying into DIA and having to drive. The major highways that people use to travel to ski areas are labeled, and other relevant roads are depicted.
